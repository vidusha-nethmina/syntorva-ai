01 / Our purpose

Intelligence is better together.

Fig. 01 Fragments becoming a network

Every institution holds decades of expertise. Too often it sits in reports, inboxes and the memory of a few specialists, and the people who need it can't reach it in time.

  1. i.

    Fragmented knowledge

    Research, guidance and records live in separate places and formats.

  2. ii.

    Limited resources

    Small expert teams cannot answer every question personally.

  3. iii.

    Repetitive enquiries

    The same questions arrive again and again, taking time from deeper work.

  4. iv.

    Hard-to-reach expertise

    Specialist knowledge rarely reaches the people in the field who need it most.

  5. v.

    Limits of conventional AI

    A single general chatbot can sound confident without grounding in trusted sources.

ZynAIde connects an institution's own knowledge with coordinated, specialised AI, so answers are grounded in sources it trusts, reviewed before they're delivered, and passed to human experts when they should be.

02 / The intelligence

Four minds. One intelligence.

Instead of one model trying to do everything, ZynAIde's planned architecture gives each step to a specialist. Each agent checks the work of the one before it, the way a good research team does.

    04 / Our innovations

    Intelligence, built for the real world.

    ZynAIde's products are separate applications, each built for one field with its own specialists. The first one is for coconut growers.

    First Pilot / In Development

    Product 01

    ZynAIde Coco

    Intelligence That Takes Root.

    ZynAIde Coco is our first specialised AI product, being developed for the Coconut Research Institute (CRI) of Sri Lanka. It aims to support coconut growers by combining AI-assisted disease assessment with research-based agricultural knowledge.

    • Coconut leaf image analysis
    • AI-assisted disease identification
    • CRI knowledge-based assistance
    • Research-grounded responses
    • Practical agricultural guidance
    • Expert referral when the system is uncertain

    All capabilities listed are planned for the pilot and still in development.

      06 / Our principles

      Innovation with responsibility.

      Technology should support informed decisions, not replace human judgement.

      1. 01

        Evidence-grounded responses

        Answers are built from identified sources so people can see where guidance comes from.

      2. 02

        Transparency and accountability

        We're clear about what the system is, what it's for and who is responsible for it.

      3. 03

        Data privacy

        We collect only what a task needs and treat institutional and personal data with care.

      4. 04

        Secure system design

        Security is part of the architecture from the first design decision.

      5. 05

        Human oversight

        Experts stay in the loop, and uncertain cases are referred to people.

      6. 06

        Responsible development

        We test carefully, document honestly and consider ethical impact throughout.

      7. 07

        Recognising limitations

        AI can be wrong. We design for that, saying so plainly and making the limits visible.
