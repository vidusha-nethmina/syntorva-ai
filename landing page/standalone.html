Platform Architecture

More Than an AI Assistant.
A Smarter Way to Connect Knowledge.

Single-prompt AI bots frequently hallucinate or generate unsafe recommendations. ZYNAIDE is an intelligent agent deployment and orchestration platform designed to distribute complex reasoning across specialised AI agents, grounding every diagnosis in vetted scientific literature.

01

The ZYNAIDE Platform Engine

Autonomous Multi-Agent Orchestration

ZYNAIDE isolates prompt inference from decision governance. Instead of asking one model to guess everything, our platform orchestrates four independent agents with distinct responsibilities, safety audits, and deterministic verification protocols.

02

Pilot Product: ZYNAIDE COCO

Coconut Research Institute (CRI) Context

Our pilot product addresses coconut tree health, pest management, and nutrient deficiencies. Users describe symptoms and upload photographs. The system uses multimodal Gemini Vision and the approved CRI knowledge base to provide transparent, verifiable answers.

Safety Audit & Escalate
Research Integrity Standard
Every diagnosis is anchored directly in vetted Coconut Research Institute publications.

When visual signs are ambiguous or treatments carry phytotoxicity risks, the system abstains from conjecture and immediately refers cases to accredited agronomists.

Orchestration Pipeline

From a Simple Inquiry to an Informed Response.

The step-by-step workflow orchestrated by ZYNAIDE in seconds whenever an inquiry is submitted.

Stage 01 of 06 User Input Intake

1. User submits a coconut-related inquiry and optionally uploads an image.

Growers, extension officers, or estate managers describe visible abnormalities (e.g. leaf yellowing, nut fall, spear rotting) along with tree age and soil context.

The Coordinator Agent receives the initial payload, validates image integrity and resolution, and assigns an asynchronous tracking job ticket for multi-agent execution.
